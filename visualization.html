💡 Key Insight

Despite dramatically different world events—Cold War, Vietnam, 9/11, Financial Crisis, COVID—the amount of crisis language remains remarkably stable. Candidates consistently use 3-4 crisis words per 1,000 words, regardless of actual world conditions.

🔄 The Eternal Themes: Same Worries, Every Generation

💡 Key Insight

The same five themes dominate every single debate for 60 years: Economy, Healthcare, Security, Education, and Foreign Policy. Your grandparents worried about the exact same things. Your grandchildren will too.

⚖️ The Fear/Hope Balance

The Story: You've Heard This All Before

Every election cycle, we're told that THIS TIME is different. This is the most important election of our lifetime. We face unprecedented challenges. The other side will destroy everything.

But the data reveals something remarkable: the pattern itself is the most predictable thing in American politics.

From Kennedy vs Nixon debating Soviet threats in 1960, to Biden vs Trump debating pandemic response in 2020, candidates use almost identical amounts of fear-based language. The average is stable. The standard deviation is low. "Unprecedented" is, ironically, the most predictable word.

What does this mean for you?

The republic has survived 60+ years of "existential threats" and "unprecedented crises." Every generation believed they faced unique, world-ending challenges. Every generation was wrong—not because the challenges weren't real, but because humanity is remarkably resilient.

The candidates need you scared. The data says you can relax.

🕊️ Breathe. You are safe. This too shall pass.
